01 / ABOUT

From atoms to algorithms.

I am Dr. Prashik Sunil Gaikwad, an Assistant Professor in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, India.

My research sits at the intersection of computational materials science, atomistic simulation and artificial intelligence. I use density functional theory, molecular dynamics, machine-learning force fields and data-driven methods to understand materials and accelerate their discovery.

My work spans energy-storage materials, polymer nanocomposites, electrochemical interfaces, ion transport and emerging quantum–classical approaches for materials simulation.

Research directions

Developing computational approaches for materials that matter — from batteries and interfaces to advanced polymers and AI-enabled discovery.

RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT

Reactive atomistic simulation of electrochemical materials

My work on eReaxFF connects quantum-mechanical reference data with large-scale reactive molecular dynamics to study oxygen vacancies, water splitting, proton migration and hydrogen generation in solid-oxide materials.
