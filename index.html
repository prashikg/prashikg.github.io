About

I am a computational materials scientist interested in developing computational and machine-learning approaches for understanding materials and their properties.

My research combines density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics (MD), machine learning, and atomistic simulations.

  • Molecular Dynamics
  • Machine Learning for Materials
  • Battery Materials
  • Electrochemical Interfaces

Research

Computational Materials Science

Development and application of atomistic simulation methods to understand structure-property relationships in materials.

Machine Learning for Materials

Development and application of machine-learning potentials, graph neural networks, and data-driven approaches for atomistic materials simulations.

Battery Materials

Computational investigation of lithium, sodium, and solid-state battery materials, interfaces, and degradation mechanisms.

Electrochemical Interfaces

Multiscale modeling of electrode-electrolyte interfaces, adsorption, electrodeposition, and electrochemical processes.
