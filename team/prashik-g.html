PROFILE

Researcher. Educator. Computational scientist.

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore. My research focuses on developing and applying computational methods to understand materials and accelerate their discovery.

My toolkit spans density functional theory, molecular dynamics, machine-learning force fields, graph neural networks and data-driven materials design. Current interests include battery materials, electrochemical interfaces, polymers and advanced computational approaches that bridge quantum and classical descriptions.
